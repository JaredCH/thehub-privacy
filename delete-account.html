Delete your TheHub account

Last updated 11 November 2026

You can delete your TheHub account and everything attached to it. There are two ways, and neither requires contacting us first.

In the app

  1. Sign in at TheHub.
  2. Open Your families.
  3. Scroll to the Delete account card.
  4. Type your password, then choose Delete my account forever.

Your login, key backups, location data, and sessions on every device are removed immediately. Families where you were the only member are deleted with you; in a family with other members, ownership passes to whoever has been there longest, and the family keeps working for them.

By email

If you cannot sign in, write to Hicksjaredc@gmail.com from any address and include your TheHub username. We will delete the account and everything attached to it within seven days, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your username, display name, password hash, and colour.
  • Every raw location point (24 hours) and every encrypted trip summary (14 days) still stored.
  • Your key backups and sessions on every device.
  • Any connected Google Calendar token, revoked with Google.
  • Families where you were the only member, including their calendar feeds and to-dos.

Server logs that mention a hashed address expire on their own within about thirty days and are not tied to your username.

What is not deleted

Nothing that identifies you. There is no backup copy kept after deletion beyond the managed database backups that expire on their normal cycle (about thirty days), and those are never restored selectively.

TheHub is a private family app. This page is referenced from the Google Play and Amazon Appstore listings and from the app's own Privacy screen.
