TheHub privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Last updated 11 November 2026

TheHub is a private family app: a live location map, shared calendars, and a 28-hour weather strip. This page explains what it does with your information.

The hosted version of this page is the authoritative one. The same text is shown inside the app at Privacy, and it is the text the Google Play and Amazon Appstore listings point at.

The short version

  • You sign up with a username and a password. We never ask for an email address or a phone number.
  • Your location is encrypted on your own device with a key your family holds. The server stores ciphertext it cannot read, and deletes raw points within 24 hours.
  • Trip summaries — where you drove, kept for 14 days — are encrypted the same way, are visible only to your family, and you can delete them at any time.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and nothing is ever sold.

What we collect and why

Your account

A username, the display name and colour you choose, a password hash (Argon2id — we never store the password itself), and a random salt used by your browser to wrap your family keys. This is what lets you sign in and be recognised by your family.

Location, only while you turn it on

When you tap Share my location, your device sends a point at most every fifteen seconds while you are moving. Each point is sealed with AES-256-GCM using your family's key before it leaves the device. We store the sealed payload plus the time, accuracy, battery percentage, whether the device is charging, and whether you were moving. We cannot decrypt any of it; the map in someone else's browser can, because they hold the same key.

Raw points are deleted from our database within 24 hours of being recorded. Your device also builds a trip summary — the route, distance, and how long you drove, moved, idled, or charged — sealed with the same key and kept for 14 days, so the family map can show the last two weeks. Everyone in the family can see those trips; that is the product, and the sharing screen says so before you start. You can delete your history at any time from the Locations card, or turn trip history off so new ones are not stored.

Turning sharing off stops new points immediately. In a browser, sharing stops when the tab is closed or hidden. In the Android app, sharing keeps recording with the screen off — that is what makes the two-week history complete — with a notification on screen the whole time and a pause control. We still do not request background location permission: the service is foreground location access, not background access.

Your family

Family name, timezone, who is a member, their role, and the invite codes that were used. Invite codes are stored only as a one-way hash plus the family key sealed with the invite secret.

Calendars

If you add a calendar feed, we store the URL encrypted and fetch it on your behalf. If you connect Google Calendar, Google gives us a read-only refresh token, which we store encrypted with a server key, and we copy event titles, times, and locations into our cache so the calendar opens quickly. Calendar content is not end-to-end encrypted: the server can read it in order to merge feeds. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes the token with Google.

Weather and maps

The weather strip asks Open-Meteo for a forecast using your position rounded to about 11 km; we store nothing from that request. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap directly from your browser, so their servers see your IP address and the area you are looking at, as they would on any map website.

Security logs

Sessions record a hashed IP address, a browser user agent, and timestamps, and our server keeps ordinary request logs. We use these to keep accounts safe and to debug failures, not to profile anyone.

What we do not collect

No email address, no phone number, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifier, no cross-site tracking, no analytics SDK, and no third-party advertising. There is no data broker on the other end of this service.

Who else touches your data

  • Railway hosts the application and its PostgreSQL database. Their privacy policy: https://railway.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google — only if you connect Google Calendar. Their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We request the calendar.readonly scope and nothing else.
  • Open-Meteo — the weather forecast. Their privacy policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — the map imagery. Their privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train anything.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies: we process account and family data because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for; security logs because we have a legitimate interest in keeping accounts safe; and location sharing and Google Calendar connections only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning the feature off.

How long we keep it

  • Raw location points: 24 hours.
  • Encrypted trip summaries (the 14-day history): 14 days, deletable at any time.
  • Calendar cache: until you remove the feed or the family.
  • Account and family data: until you delete your account or leave the family.
  • Server logs: about thirty days.
  • Deleting your account removes your login, key backups, and location data immediately.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Location and calendar credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; family keys are derived with HKDF-SHA256 and never sent to the server; passwords are hashed with Argon2id; sessions are opaque random tokens, stored only as hashes. No system is perfect, but a database dump of TheHub does not contain anyone's location.

Your choices

  • Change your display name or colour at any time.
  • Pause sharing, or leave a family, from the family page.
  • Delete your trip history, or turn it off so new trips are not stored, from the Locations card.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar from the calendar panel.
  • Delete your account in the app (Your families → Delete account) or request it at the delete page. Families you were the only member of are deleted; in a family with other members, ownership passes to the longest-standing member.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by writing to Hicksjaredc@gmail.com.

Children

TheHub is made for families, but an account holder must be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

A parent or guardian may choose to install TheHub on a device used by their child, or to share that device's location with the family. That decision — and any consent required in your country for a child's location to be shared — is theirs, not ours. We do not advertise to anyone, we do not build profiles, and we do not use location for anything except showing it to your family.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and the new version is published at this same URL.
